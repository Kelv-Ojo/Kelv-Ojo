# Hi, I'm Kelvin

I'm building a career in **data analysis**, with projects that turn raw data into clear reports, useful comparisons and practical questions for a business to investigate.

My portfolio focuses on **SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI**, with attention to metric definitions, data quality and communicating limitations.

## Featured projects

### [Hotel Booking Analysis](https://github.com/Kelv-Ojo/hotel-booking-analysis)

**Question:** Where should a hotel team investigate cancellations?

A case study of 119,390 historical bookings. Includes a five-table SQL model, joins and window functions, independent reconciliation, and an Excel report with year/hotel filters and monthly/channel comparisons. My earlier Power BI dashboard is preserved with a documented audit and rebuild specification.

## How I approach analysis

- Start with a decision and define the metrics and reporting period.
- Check missing values, keys, dates and units before interpreting results.
- Reconcile aggregates and explain assumptions.
- Communicate findings, suggested next steps and what the data cannot prove.

These are portfolio and educational projects developed with AI assistance. Each project explains its sources, methods and limitations; no client engagement or realized business impact is claimed. Power BI and Excel refresh steps that have not been tested in their desktop applications are identified in the project documentation.
